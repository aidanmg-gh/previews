Questions

The ones owners ask most.

Does my website matter if my reviews are already good?

Yes. After reading positive reviews, 54% of people check the business's website (BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey, February 2026). In BrightLocal's August 2026 study of AI search answers about local businesses, about 93% of the websites cited were individual business sites. Your reviews bring people in. Your site is where they decide.

Will this put me first on Google?

No one can promise that, and I won't. I promise a site that is fast, clear, accurate and easy to call from.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and you own every word and photograph. On either plan you can ask for all the files whenever you want.

What if I want to leave?

On Managed, cancel any time with 30 days' notice and I hand over all the files. On Own it, the site is already yours and hosted on your account.

Do you replace my logo and colors?

No. Your logo, your colors and anything that already works are kept. The rest is rebuilt.

What do you need from me?

Your photographs, your license number and the towns you serve. I collect your reviews from your public Google listing.

Why not hire an agency?

With an agency you talk to an account manager. With me you talk to the person building your site, from the first call through launch and after. About the founder.
