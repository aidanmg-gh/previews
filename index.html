Services

A website built around how customers actually hire a contractor.

Most customers find a contractor on a phone, read a few reviews, and place a call. The website should make that take seconds.

  • Tap to call on the first screen of every page, with a call bar that stays visible on phones.
  • Google reviews presented word for word, with the star rating and a link to the business listing.
  • Real photographs of the crew, the trucks and the completed work. No stock images.
  • A page for each main service, a project gallery, and a contact form that delivers each request directly to the business.
  • The CSLB license number on every page, and a privacy page, prepared to follow California's rules for contractor advertising and websites.
  • Fast and clean. No pop-ups, no tracking, no chat widgets. Each site is checked with Google's speed and accessibility tools before launch.
  • Continuity of identity. The existing logo, colors and any elements that already work are kept. The rest is rebuilt.

Process

Approximately one week, from agreement to launch.

  1. Agreement. A short written agreement in plain English.
  2. Materials. The client provides photographs, the license number and the towns served. Straightedge Web collects the reviews from the public Google listing.
  3. First version. Delivered in about a week, with two rounds of revisions before launch.
  4. Launch. Straightedge Web connects the domain and the contact form.
