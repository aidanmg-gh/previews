Six things, on every site.

Most customers find a contractor on a phone, read a few reviews, and call. The site should make that take seconds. The numbers match the drawing above.

  1. A call button on the first screen

    One tap, no scrolling. The same number sits in the call bar and the footer of every page.

  2. Your reviews, word for word

    Your Google reviews quoted exactly as written, with the star rating and a link to your listing. After reading good reviews, 54% of people visit the business's website (BrightLocal, 2026). Yours should be worth the visit.

  3. Your photos, not stock

    Your crew, your trucks, your finished jobs. No stranger in a hard hat.

  4. Your license number on every page

    California requires it on contractor advertising, websites included (CSLB Advertising Guidelines). I check your license on the state's public record before I build anything.

  5. A call and text bar that stays put

    On phones, Call and Text stay at the bottom of the screen while people scroll.

  6. Your domain, your files

    The domain is registered in your name. You get every file whenever you ask. No contract that traps you.
