Signs it's the main line

One slow drain is usually just that drain. When several back up at once, the problem is further down.

  • More than one drain clogged at the same time
  • Water coming up in the tub when the toilet flushes
  • Gurgling from drains or toilets
  • Sewage smell inside or in the yard
  • A wet, soggy patch over where the sewer line runs

What we use

The right tool depends on what's in the line, so we look before we guess.

Camera inspection
A camera on a cable goes down the line and shows roots, cracks, sags and blockages, so you see what we see.
Drain cleaning
A cable machine for everyday clogs in sinks, tubs, toilets and laundry lines.
Hydro jetting
High-pressure water that scours grease, scale and roots off the inside of the pipe, not just a hole through the clog.
Sewer line repair
Fixing or replacing broken sections once the camera shows where they are.
