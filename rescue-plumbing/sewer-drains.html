Sewer backing up?

If water comes up in the tub or shower when you flush, or more than one drain is slow at the same time, the trouble is usually in the main sewer line, not a single drain. Stop running water and call (818) 510-1204, day or night.

Signs your sewer line needs attention

  • Several slow drains at once
  • Gurgling from toilets or drains
  • Water backing up in the lowest drain in the house
  • A sewer smell inside, or in the yard
  • Wet, sunken or extra-green patches over the line

Video camera line inspection

Instead of guessing, we run a camera down the line to see what's actually wrong: tree roots, a grease clog, a crack or a sag in the pipe. Then you get a flat, upfront price for the fix.

Drain cleaning and hydro jetting

Most clogs clear with a drain cable. For heavy grease, scale and tree roots, hydro jetting scours the inside of the pipe with high-pressure water, so the line runs clean instead of just getting a hole punched through the clog.

First-time customers get $50 off sewer or drain cleaning.

Sewer line repair

When a section of pipe is broken, crushed or full of roots, cleaning won't hold. We dig down to the damaged section and repair it, then test the line before it's covered back up.
