No hot water? Call us first.

Most water heater calls start the same way: a cold shower, a puddle on the garage floor, or a pilot light that won't stay lit. Call (818) 510-1204, tell us what you're seeing, and you'll get a flat, upfront price before any work starts.

Water heater repair

We repair tank and tankless water heaters. The calls we get most often:

  • Leaks from the tank, a valve or a connection
  • No hot water, or hot water that runs out too fast
  • A gas pilot or burner that won't light or stay lit
  • A relief valve that drips or keeps letting go
  • A tank that rumbles, pops or bangs while it heats

Replacing a water heater

A tank that leaks from the bottom can't be fixed, and an old heater that keeps breaking usually costs more to nurse along than to replace. We'll tell you plainly which one you're looking at.

Every new water heater in California has to meet code: seismic straps, a drain pan where one is required, and a relief valve piped to a safe spot. Those aren't extras. They're part of doing the job right.

Tankless water heaters

A tankless heater warms water as you use it, so a long shower doesn't run it dry, and it hangs on the wall instead of taking up a closet. We install new tankless units, replace old ones and repair the ones you have.

Switching from a tank to tankless often needs a bigger gas line from the meter. We run that too, so one plumber handles the whole job.
