About us

Old fashioned service that is hard to find in the modern world.

"Doing business on a handshake" and "I give you my word" are long gone. This is not true with Voltech Electric. Over the years, we have earned the reputation of providing old fashioned service that is hard to find in the modern world. No matter what your job may call for, we are there to help. We specialize in both residential and commercial electrical work and troubleshooting.

We install new electric service panels, starting from 100 amps and up. New circuits, recessed lighting, ceiling fans, smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, exterior lighting, motion detectors, A/C circuits, bathroom exhaust fans and GFCI protected outlets.

We replace outlets and switches, burned or broken breakers, fluorescent light fixtures, and much more.

Our mission

Some suggest those high standards can become harder to maintain as organizations grow, when the standards of an electrician and the business come in conflict. But do they? The very heart of our business is providing our clients with high-quality electrical structures, constructed safely, and finished on time. It is not just the finish that reflects craftsmanship, it is the whole project, and every aspect of the project.

We believe this is about how you first approach your work, the goals you set for its execution, and the objective of being able to look at the work you have accomplished, whether it is a drawing, a specification, or rewiring an entire home, and being proud of the finished product. It is about maintaining a high standard of quality in the work each of us does, always endeavoring to do it most efficiently, but without the shortcuts that affect value and quality.
