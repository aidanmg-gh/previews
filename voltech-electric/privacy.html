Privacy policy

Short version: we only use your information to answer you and do your job. We don't sell it, and this site doesn't track you.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Voltech Electric Inc website. Voltech Electric Inc is based in Sunland, California, CSLB Lic. #1018757 (C-10 Electrical). Questions: call 818-482-4563 or email voltechelectric@aol.com.

What we collect

  • The quote form: your name, phone number, city, the kind of work you need, and any notes you add.
  • Calls and email: your phone number or email address and what you tell us.

We don't collect anything else through this site.

How we use it

Only to answer your request, give you a quote, schedule and do the work, and follow up on that job.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. It's only handled by the companies that run this website and deliver the form to us, and only so the site works. We'll share it beyond that only if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, chat widgets or other tracking. It doesn't let other companies collect information about what you do over time or across other websites. Links to Google, Yelp or the CSLB take you to their sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Do Not Track

Because this site doesn't track you, it works the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your information

Call 818-482-4563 to ask what we have, correct it, or have it deleted, unless we need to keep it for a job record or the law.

Children

This site is for homeowners and businesses. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
