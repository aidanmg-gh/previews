How to find and hire an electrician

If you are having electrical problems in your home or office, it is important to hire a qualified electrician. Not all of them have the skills and experience your job needs. Here's a quick guide.

  1. Understand your electrical needs

    Know what kind of work you need: a new installation, a repair, an upgrade, or an inspection. It helps you find someone with the right skills.

  2. Check the license

    In California, look the electrician up on the Contractors State License Board website.

    Ours: CSLB Lic. #1018757 (C-10 Electrical)

  3. Check reviews and ratings

    Read what earlier customers say about reliability, punctuality and the quality of the work.

    Ours: 5.0 on Google from 41 reviews

  4. Ask for references

    Ask for references from earlier clients. They give you a better sense of the work and the customer service.

  5. Check credentials and experience

    Ask about qualifications, certifications and years of experience, so you know they can handle your job.

  6. Discuss the scope of work

    Go over the timeline, the budget and anything specific to your job. Be sure you are comparing apples to apples.
