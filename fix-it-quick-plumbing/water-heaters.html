Signs you need it

Not every problem means a new heater. Sometimes it's a part, and we'll tell you if it is.

  • Water around the base of the tank.
  • Hot water runs out faster than it used to, or never gets hot.
  • Rust-colored water from the hot tap.
  • You want to switch to tankless.

Tank or tankless

We install both.

Tank
The standard heater in a closet or garage. A straight swap is usually the quickest fix when the old one fails.
Tankless
Heats water as you use it, mounted on a wall inside or out. Switching usually means changes to the gas line and venting, which we handle too.
