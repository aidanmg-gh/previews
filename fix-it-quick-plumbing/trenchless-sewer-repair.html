What is trenchless sewer repair?

Trenchless repair fixes or replaces a sewer line without digging a trench across your property. We work through small access points instead, so your landscaping, driveway and floors stay where they are.

How the trenchless process works

  1. Camera inspection. We run a video camera through your sewer line to find the blockage or damage exactly.
  2. We pick the method. Once we know the problem, we use one of two methods: pipe bursting or pipe lining.
  3. The fix. The new pipe or liner goes in through the access points, and the line is back in service.

Pipe bursting

A bursting head breaks apart the old pipe while it pulls the new one into place behind it, along the same path. It keeps the line's structure sound, can increase flow, and extends the life of your piping.

Pipe lining and epoxy pipe lining

Lining reinforces the pipe you already have. A durable epoxy liner goes inside the old line and hardens into a new pipe within the pipe.
