Trenchless sewer repair in Canoga Park and the Valley

A new sewer line without a trench across your yard. We work through small access pits and either pull a new pipe through the old one or line the old one from the inside.

Signs you need it

A single clog doesn't mean a new sewer line. Often a cleaning or a spot repair is enough, and the camera will show that.

  • The main line keeps backing up, even after it's been cleaned.
  • More than one drain in the house is slow or gurgling at once.
  • A camera inspection shows roots, cracks or a collapsed section.
  • Sewage smell or wet patches in the yard over the line.

Two ways to do it

Which one fits depends on what the camera shows.

Pipe bursting
A bursting head breaks the old pipe apart while it pulls a new pipe, usually high-density polyethylene (HDPE), into the same path. Good for lines that are collapsed or too far gone to save.
Epoxy pipe lining
The old pipe is cleaned, then coated inside with epoxy resin that cures into a smooth new lining. It seals cracks and gaps and keeps your existing line in place.

How a job goes

  1. Camera inspection.We run a camera down the line so you can see the problem.
  2. Options and a price.Cleaning, spot repair, lining or bursting, with what each one costs.
  3. Small access pits.Dug where the line needs to be reached, not along its whole length.
  4. New line in, pits filled.The new pipe or lining goes in and the pits are closed back up.
