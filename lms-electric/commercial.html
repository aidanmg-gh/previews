Generator: Emergency Response

We are on standby!

LMS Electric provides temporary utility power, standby services and emergency response. We can be wherever you need us, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

We are equipped with two 500kW generators and growing. We come prepared with a trailer containing all the cabling you will need and an electrician ready, so you can be up and running in no time. We are on standby to make sure any losses from an outage are kept to a minimum.
