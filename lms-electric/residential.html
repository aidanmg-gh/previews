Panel Upgrades & Replacements

When troubleshooting electrical issues in your home, the first place to look is the breaker box. If a breaker trips often or won’t stay reset, it may need to be replaced. Call us right away if your breakers are hot to the touch, give off a burning smell, show black or burned material, or you notice exposed, frayed wires.

Generator Transfer Switch

Don’t be left without power. A transfer switch gives your generator a dedicated set of switches for each circuit you want to power, so during an outage or after an earthquake you can keep the water heater, phones and car charging going.

Lighting Retrofit

LED bulbs use up to 90% less energy than standard bulbs and give the same light. We swap old fixtures and bulbs for efficient LEDs in every style and size.

Lighting Installation & Design

No interior design project is complete without lighting. Light up the details inside your home, add curb appeal outside, and make a narrow hallway feel wider or a small bedroom feel more spacious.

Energy Efficiency

LMS Electric offers FREE energy efficiency consultations to help you cut what your home uses.

Fan Installation

Ceiling, attic and exhaust fans. A ceiling fan can make a room feel cooler, so you can set the thermostat higher and save on your electric bill.

Electrical Code Correction

Code violations should be fixed right away by a qualified electrician. We handle faulty panels, incorrectly sized wiring, safety violations, overloaded circuits, hardware upgrades, damaged cables, poor connections and abandoned electrical projects, and get you up to code.

Property Management

An experienced, prompt team for property managers: installation, repair, inspection or emergency, at any time. No job too big or too small.
