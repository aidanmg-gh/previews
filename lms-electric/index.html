Questions

Do you offer same day service?

Yes. Same Day Service, 7 days a week, 24/7. The office is open 8am to 5pm, Monday to Friday, and the emergency line, 818-391-9850, is answered around the clock.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Call or send the form and we'll set up a free estimate, or a service call if you need someone out now.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Montrose and serve the San Fernando Valley, San Gabriel Valley and Central Los Angeles, with services available throughout Southern California.

Do you work on homes, or just businesses?

Both. We maintain over 100 Ralphs and Food 4 Less stores, the Rose Bowl and LAFC's headquarters, and we're here for homeowners and property management companies too, with 24/7 residential service.

Can you bring power during an outage?

Yes. We provide temporary utility power, standby service and emergency response, with two 500kW generators, a trailer with all the cabling, and an electrician to hook it up.

Is there a discount for returning customers?

Yes. Tell your LMS electrician while they're on site and save 10%, up to $250. Special restrictions may apply; not valid with other offers.

Are you licensed?

Yes. CSLB Lic. #887117 (B, C-10, C-36). You can check it on the CSLB website.
