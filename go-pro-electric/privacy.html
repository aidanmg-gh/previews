Privacy policy

Short version: I only use your information to answer you and do your job. I don't sell it, and this site doesn't track you.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Go Pro Electric website. Go Pro Electric is owned by Justin Williams, Murrieta, California, CSLB License #1099773. Questions: call or text (951) 387-6593.

What I collect

  • The estimate form: your name, phone number, city, the kind of work you need, any notes, and any photo you choose to upload.
  • Calls and texts: your phone number and what you tell me.

I don't collect anything else through this site.

How I use it

Only to answer your request, give you an estimate, schedule and do the work, and follow up on that job.

Who I share it with

I don't sell or rent your information. It's only handled by the companies that run this website and deliver the form to me, and only so the site works. I'll share it beyond that only if the law requires it.

Texts

If you text me, I'll text you back about your job. I don't send marketing texts. Reply STOP any time and I'll stop texting you.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking. It doesn't let other companies collect information about what you do over time or across other websites.

Do Not Track

Because this site doesn't track you, it works the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your information

Call or text (951) 387-6593 to ask what I have, correct it, or have it deleted, unless I need to keep it for a job record or the law.

Children

This site is for homeowners and businesses. It isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
