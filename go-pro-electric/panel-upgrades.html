Signs you need one

Not every problem means a new panel. I'll tell you if a breaker swap or a new circuit will do.

  • Breakers trip when the AC, microwave or dryer kicks on
  • There's no room left in the panel for a new breaker
  • You're adding an EV charger, hot tub, spa, AC or a room
  • Lights dim or flicker when big appliances start
  • The panel is old, rusted, or still has fuses

How a panel upgrade goes

Panel work in California needs a permit and an inspection. Here's the order of things.

  1. Free estimate.I look at your panel and what you want to add, and give you a price.
  2. Permit.The upgrade is permitted with your city or the county.
  3. Upgrade day.Power is off for part of the day while the new panel goes in.
  4. Inspection.The building inspector checks the work and signs off.

Panel questions

Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade?

Yes. In California, panel replacements and upgrades are permitted and inspected.

Will my power be off?

For part of the day, while the old panel comes out and the new one goes in.
