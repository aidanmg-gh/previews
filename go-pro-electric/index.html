When it comes to electricity, experience matters

My name is Justin Williams and I am the owner of Go Pro Electric. I have been in the electrical trade for over 20 years, as a journeyman wireman and as an electrical foreman overseeing numerous projects. From troubleshooting to new residential construction to large commercial facilities, I have done it all.

Before obtaining my C-10 contractor's license and starting Go Pro Electric, I worked as a journeyman wireman for the largest electrical union in the world, on projects such as John Wayne International Airport, Apple Campus, Camp Pendleton Naval Hospital, FBI field offices, Kaiser Hospitals and numerous universities and schools.

After so many years in the industry, often traveling all over the state for weeks at a time, I made the decision to start Go Pro Electric so that I can be present with my children and family. I am thankful for each of my clients for their support, and I will always strive to provide the best service at competitive rates.
