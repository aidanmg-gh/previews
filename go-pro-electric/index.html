What I do

Most calls start with something that stopped working, or a project you've been putting off. I explain your options before I start, and you get a straight price.

Panel upgrades
Main panel replacements and upgrades, new breakers, more capacity for EV chargers, spas and additions. More on panel upgrades →
EV chargers
Tesla and other Level 2 chargers, plus the circuit or outlet to run them. More on EV chargers →
Lighting and ceiling fans
Recessed and canless lights, pendants, chandeliers, dimmers and ceiling fans.

Outdoor
