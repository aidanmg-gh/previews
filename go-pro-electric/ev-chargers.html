Charger or outlet?

Two ways to charge at home. I'll help you pick before you spend money.

Wall charger
A Tesla Wall Connector or another Level 2 charger, wired straight to its own circuit. Clean install, and the fastest charging at home.
240-volt outlet
A dedicated outlet your car's mobile charger plugs into. Useful if you might change cars later.
Panel check
Both need room in your panel. If there isn't enough, a panel upgrade comes first.
