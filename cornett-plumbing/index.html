About Cornett Plumbing

Cornett Plumbing & Heating Inc. was founded in 1972 by two brothers in the South Bay Area. We are a third-generation business that is still family owned and operated today, and we treat our customers like family.

Cornett Plumbing in Torrance is a premier residential plumbing and heating company. If it's plumbing or heating, we have the experience to complete the job. Our entire staff has years of experience in the industry, backed with certifications and extensive training in plumbing, heating and other services, such as tankless water heaters. We conduct background checks and drug screening on our staff to guarantee the safety of our customers.
