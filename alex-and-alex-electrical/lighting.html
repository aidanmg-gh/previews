Planning it

Recessed lights are a layout problem before they're a wiring problem. We'll help you work out how many, where, and how far apart.

  • Rooms that only have one ceiling fixture, or none.
  • Old fluorescent tubes in a kitchen, garage or shop.
  • Shelves, bars and display cases that need light in them.

How a job goes

  1. We walk the room with you.Which lights, how many, and where they go.
  2. Small holes.We cut only what the job needs, so the patch-up is easier.
  3. Dimmers and switches set up before we leave.
