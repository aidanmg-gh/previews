Signs you need it

A breaker that trips once in a while doesn't mean you need a new panel. Call and describe it first.

  • Your panel is a Zinsco, Stab-Lok (Federal Pacific) or Pushmatic.
  • You're adding an EV charger, an ADU, a garage conversion or a big appliance and the panel is full.
  • Breakers that run hot, buzz, or keep tripping on normal use.

How a job goes

Every panel upgrade is permitted and inspected.

  1. We look at what you have.The panel, the meter and what you want to run on it.
  2. You get the price.
  3. Permit, install, inspection.
