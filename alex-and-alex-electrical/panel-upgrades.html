Panel Upgrades

200 amp main panel upgrades from dangerous panels like Zinsco, Stab-Lok and Pushmatic. Permitted and inspected.

When people call us about a panel

  • The house still has a Zinsco, Stab-Lok or Pushmatic panel.
  • You're adding a Tesla or other EV charger and the panel is full.
  • An ADU, a garage conversion or a remodel needs more power.
  • Breakers keep tripping, or the panel is old and nobody knows what's on which circuit.

What you get

  • A 200 amp main panel.
  • The work is permitted and inspected.
  • A family team: father and step-son, both named Alex. English, Russian and Hebrew.
