Signs it's time

One leak doesn't mean you need a new roof. If the rest of it is sound, a repair costs less.

  • Leaks in more than one place, or the same spot leaking again after repairs
  • Shingles that are curling, cracked or losing their granules across the roof
  • Lots of broken or slipped tiles, or the underlayment under the tile is worn out
  • You're selling, and the inspection report flagged the roof
