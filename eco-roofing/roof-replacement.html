Is your roof showing signs of aging, damage, or constant repair needs? Then it may be time for a new one. A new roof protects your home, adds to its value, and gives you peace of mind for years to come.

Replacing a roof is a big investment. That's why we give you straight guidance, transparent pricing and good service from the first visit to the last nail.

Roof replacement options

Asphalt Shingle

Affordable, durable, and available in many colours and styles. The most popular choice for Los Angeles homes.

Tile

Timeless and durable, and right at home on many Southern California styles of house.

Metal

Known for a long life and energy efficiency, for owners who want long-term value.

Flat

Flat roof replacements for homes and commercial buildings, fixing ponding water and membrane failure for good.

Signs you may need a new roof

  • Your roof is 20+ years old
  • Leaks that keep coming back
  • Missing, curling or cracked shingles
  • Granule loss on asphalt shingles
  • Sagging or soft spots on the roof deck
  • Rising energy bills from poor insulation
  • Daylight showing through the attic

If you see any of these, book an inspection. We'll look at the roof and tell you honestly whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense.

Our roof replacement process

  1. Free roof inspection and consultation

    We inspect the roof, explain your options, answer your questions and give you a detailed estimate.

  2. Material selection

    Shingle, metal, tile or a flat roof system. We help you choose the style, colour and material for your home and your budget.

  3. Professional installation

    Our roofers handle the whole installation and build it to meet local building codes.

  4. Cleanup and final inspection

    We clean up fully and do a final inspection before we leave.
