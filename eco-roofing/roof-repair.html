Signs you need it

A stain after one big storm can be a single cracked tile or a gap at the flashing. Small fixes stay small if they're done early.

  • Water stains on the ceiling, or drips during rain
  • Cracked, slipped or missing tiles or shingles
  • A flat roof with bubbles, cracks or water that sits for days
