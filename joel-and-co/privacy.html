Privacy policy

Short version: we only use your information to answer you and work on your project. We don't sell it, and this site doesn't track you.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Joel & Co. Construction website. Joel & Co. Construction, 11693 San Vicente Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90049, CSLB Lic. #967864 (B, General Building). Questions: call (310) 728-6181 or email joelandcompany@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • The project form: your name, phone number, email, city, whether the project is residential or commercial, the service you need, your budget range and what you tell us about the project.
  • Calls and emails: your phone number or email address and what you tell us.

We don't collect anything else through this site.

How we use it

Only to answer your request, prepare a proposal, schedule and do the work, and follow up on that project.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. It's only handled by the companies that run this website and deliver the form to us, and only so the site works. We'll share it beyond that only if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking. It doesn't let other companies collect information about what you do over time or across other websites.

Do Not Track

Because this site doesn't track you, it works the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your information

Call (310) 728-6181 or email us to ask what we have, correct it, or have it deleted, unless we need to keep it for a project record or the law.

Children

This site is for homeowners and businesses. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
