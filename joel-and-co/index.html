Exceptional Craftsmanship Led by a Trusted Process

  1. Contact us

    Reach out to our team for an initial consultation. From either a short phone conversation or an intake form, we can determine if we’re the right fit for the project.

  2. On-site meeting

    We will meet on-site to get a sense of the space and gather information for the build. We’ll discuss your plans, vision and overall needs for the project.

  3. Proposal

    Our team typically takes 1-2 weeks to prepare a detailed proposal for your review. This will include the full scope of the project.

  4. Proposal review & approval

    From here, you can review our proposal and ask any follow up questions or request revisions before approving. Once approved we will solidify agreements and build your project’s work schedule.

  5. Design & planning

    Through construction documentation and materials selections, we work with you throughout the design process to ensure your vision comes to life. Depending on the scope and complexity of your project, we will review floorplans, elevations, and renderings, along with selecting the perfect materials for every space.

  6. Build & implementation

    Once design and layout decisions are finalized, our team begins the construction phase and keeps you up to date with our schedule every step of the way.
