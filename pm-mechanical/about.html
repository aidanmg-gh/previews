Get to know us

The P&M Mechanical difference.

Family owned, not a franchise

P & M Mechanical is not a franchise, it is family owned and operated by Michelle Leonard. We are a local company that has been in business since 1988, with excellent references from many of your neighbors.

Your home stays clean

P & M Mechanical provides each homeowner with the assurance that your home is kept clean with drop cloths and our "No Work Boots Will Touch Your Floor" policy. There is also a daily installer's checklist to ensure nothing is forgotten.

Your home stays safe

You can also be assured that your home is always safe with our crews, as we do an extensive criminal background check on all our installers.

Trained by us

Our Comfort Specialists are factory trained, with continuing education through our HVAC equipment manufacturers and The Institute of Heating and Air Conditioning, among others. P & M Mechanical installers are all personally trained by us.

Licensed, with permits pulled

Our company carries liability and workers' compensation insurance, and we are licensed for your protection: CSLB Lic. #604936 (C-20 Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating & Air-Conditioning). P & M Mechanical will pull all the necessary city permits for your installation.

We stand behind it

P & M Mechanical guarantees all work and has a great reputation for being clean, honest, and dependable.
