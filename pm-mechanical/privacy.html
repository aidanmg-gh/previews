Privacy policy

Short version: we only use your information to answer you and do your job. We don't sell it, and this site doesn't track you.

Who this covers

This policy covers the P&M Mechanical website. P & M Mechanical Heating & Air Conditioning Specialists Inc, 23991 Arlington Ave, Torrance, CA 90501, CSLB Lic. #604936 (C-20 Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating & Air-Conditioning). Questions: call 310.530.6642 or email Info@pmmech.com.

What we collect

  • The contact form: your name, phone number, email address, the kind of work you need, and any notes.
  • Calls and emails: your phone number or email address and what you tell us.

We don't collect anything else through this site.

How we use it

Only to answer your request, give you an estimate, schedule and do the work, and follow up on that job.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. It's only handled by the companies that run this website and deliver the form to us, and only so the site works. We'll share it beyond that only if the law requires it. The financing link takes you to Synchrony's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking. It doesn't let other companies collect information about what you do over time or across other websites.

Do Not Track

Because this site doesn't track you, it works the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your information

Call 310.530.6642 or email us to ask what we have, correct it, or have it deleted, unless we need to keep it for a job record or the law.

Children

This site is for homeowners and businesses. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
