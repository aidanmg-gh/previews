Common questions

Answers to all your contracting inquiries. All questions

Do I need a city permit?

Yes. Mechanical, electrical, and sometimes plumbing permits are necessary. Any contractor failing to obtain the required building permits is violating the law and potentially exposing the homeowner to additional costs and liability. Many companies try to avoid the permit process because this allows them to cut corners, but we will not.

When do we pay you?

Payment is normally due in full upon completion of work and upon passing city inspection. Financing is available with approved credit through Synchrony.

How often should my equipment be serviced?

Our HVAC equipment manufacturers recommend yearly maintenance, to keep your equipment running at peak efficiency, prolong equipment life, and satisfy all warranty requirements. P&M Mechanical offers a Residential Energy Savings Agreement to meet your maintenance needs.

Who do we call if we have a problem with our equipment?

Call us at 310.530.6642. We are fully trained and carry an extensive inventory of parts for all the equipment we install.
