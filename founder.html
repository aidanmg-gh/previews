Founder of Straightedge Web, a Los Angeles company that builds clear, accurate websites for licensed contractors.

Why this exists.

Many Los Angeles contractors have earned hundreds of strong customer reviews, yet their websites are slow on a phone, buried in stock photography, or missing the license number California requires. Customers judge a contractor by the website before the first call.

I started Straightedge Web in 2026 to close that gap: a website as dependable as the work.

The name refers to the straightedge a tradesperson uses to mark a true line.
  • Accuracy firstReviews are quoted word for word. Claims on a site are limited to what the contractor can support, and each license is checked against the public CSLB record before any work is shown.
  • ComplianceThe license number appears on every page, and the site follows California's advertising rules for contractors.
  • Real workPhotographs come from the contractor's own jobs, crew and vehicles.
  • PerformancePages are tested for speed and accessibility before launch. No pop-ups, trackers or chat widgets.
  • OwnershipThe client keeps the domain, the content and the files, with no lock-in.
  • Direct accessEvery client works with me, from the first conversation through launch and ongoing support. No account managers and no hand-offs.
