Why I started this

I started Straightedge Web in 2026 after seeing the same thing over and over. Los Angeles contractors with hundreds of five-star Google reviews and a website that shows none of it. The site is slow on a phone, full of stock photos, or missing the license number California requires. People judge a contractor by that site before they ever call.

So I build better ones, and I keep it small on purpose. You deal with me, from the first call through launch and after. There are no account managers and no hand-offs.

I look up every license on the state's public CSLB record before I show anyone work. Reviews are quoted word for word. The photos are yours, not stock. You keep the domain, the content and the files, so you are never stuck with me.

The name comes from the straightedge a tradesperson uses to mark a true line. That is the standard I'm aiming for.
