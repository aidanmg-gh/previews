Privacy

This site collects nothing about you. Here is what that means, and what happens if you contact me.

This website

The pages have no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms. They load nothing from other websites. The email and text buttons on the home page do not send anything by themselves. They open a message to me in your own mail or messaging app, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of a visit, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement. I do not receive or use those logs.

If you email or text me
I receive what you send, including your name, number and website address. I use it only to reply and to do the work you ask for.
If we work together
I keep your project files and agreement for as long as the work and the law require, and I hand you your files when you ask.
Sharing
I do not sell your information or give it to advertisers. I share it only with services your project needs, such as your domain or hosting company, and only with your say-so.
Deleting it
Email or text me and I will delete what I hold about you, except what I must keep for tax or legal reasons.
