The work

Three demo sites for made-up contractors, each with its own look. Open them on your phone.

These are demos, not real clients. The company names, license numbers and reviews are made up. The photos are free sample photos from Unsplash, not the work of these companies, and the people in them are not these companies' staff. On your site they would be your own crew, trucks and finished jobs, along with your name, logo, colors and real reviews.

Plumbing demo

Marlowe & Sons

For a family business that wants to feel trusted and local. Warm paper color, a serif headline and a red call button, with a real photo of the work up front.

  • Call button first. The number is in a top bar, the header and the hero, with the license in a strip above everything.
  • Trust strip. Licensed, price first, on time and warrantied, in four short lines under the first screen.
  • Reviews with dates. Quoted word for word, so people can see the praise is recent.
  • Photo service cards. Each main job gets a real photo and a plain sentence.
